Understanding custovox.ai

What is custovox.ai?

custovox.ai is a French Voice of Customer analysis platform based on AI and NLP. It automatically analyzes calls, customer reviews, support tickets and emails to detect emotions, escalation risks, churn risk and pain points, without a data team.

How is custovox.ai different from a standard sentiment analysis tool?

A standard sentiment analysis tool tells you whether a review is positive or negative. custovox.ai goes further. It identifies the precise emotion, detects escalation risks and predicts churn. It analyzes voice and images as well as text, and statistically validates its results.

What types of data can custovox.ai analyze?

custovox.ai analyzes text (reviews, tickets, emails, survey verbatims), voice (calls, interviews, podcasts) and images (photos of products, claims, property inspections). These analyses are split into five modules: TextIQ, VoiceIQ, VisualIQ, PredictIQ and SearchIQ.

Which languages does custovox.ai support?

custovox.ai natively analyzes five languages, and analysis remains possible in other languages. You can compare customer feedback from several countries with the same method.

How it works

How do you automatically analyze customer reviews with AI?

With custovox.ai, you import your customer reviews, tickets or call transcripts. The TextIQ module then identifies recurring themes, emotions, pain points and unmet needs. Results are statistically validated, so you can tell a real recurring problem from an isolated case. A concrete example: what analyzing 200 G2 reviews revealed.

How do you detect customers who are about to leave (churn)?

custovox.ai spots the signals in customer conversations that come before a departure. It combines language analysis (TextIQ, VoiceIQ) with predictive modeling (PredictIQ), so you can act before the customer cancels.

How do you detect calls at risk of escalation in a call center?

custovox.ai relies on a proprietary escalation detection model, tested on real call center conversations. It identifies exchanges likely to turn into a complaint or a customer departure, so supervisors can step in in time.

Which AI models does custovox.ai use?

custovox.ai relies on its own analysis models and scripts, specialized in customer relations and fine-tuned with anonymized data. For each analysis, custovox.ai uses the best-suited model.

Do I need a data team to use custovox.ai?

No. custovox.ai is designed for customer service, marketing and quality teams with no technical skills. Analyses are delivered ready to use.

Can custovox.ai connect to my existing tools?

Yes. custovox.ai offers an API with a single key and separate modules, so you can connect your existing customer service tools.

In what format should I send my data to custovox.ai?

custovox.ai accepts all customer data formats: text files and spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, TXT), exports from your support or CRM tools, emails, audio files (MP3, WAV…) and images (JPG, PNG…). You send your data as is, with no technical preparation.

Data, security and GDPR

Does custovox.ai use my data to train its models?

No. With custovox.ai, your data is used only to produce your analyses and is never used to train models.

Where is custovox.ai data hosted?

custovox.ai is published by a French company and hosts your data in France. Analyses are run with its own scripts, without sending your data to third-party AI providers.

How does custovox.ai protect my data?

custovox.ai protects your data at every step. It is hosted in France and encrypted both in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (AES-256 encryption). Access is restricted to authorized people only, with strong authentication and access logging. Personal data (names, phone numbers, email and postal addresses) is anonymized before analysis. Finally, your data is never used to train models. Details are in our privacy policy.

How long does custovox.ai keep my data, and can I delete it?

custovox.ai keeps your data only for the duration of your subscription. You can request its deletion at any time: it is carried out within 30 days. When your subscription ends, your data is deleted within 30 days. For the free case study, it is deleted 30 days after the results are delivered.

Does custovox.ai sign a data processing agreement (DPA)?

Yes. custovox.ai signs a data processing agreement (DPA) with its customers, in line with Article 28 of the GDPR. This contract specifies how your data is processed, protected and deleted. To get one, contact us.
