What is a customer sentiment analysis tool?

A customer sentiment analysis tool is software that uses natural language processing (NLP) to automatically detect the emotional tone of the texts and conversations produced by customers: online reviews, support tickets, calls, emails, chats. It classifies each interaction as positive, negative or neutral, and advanced tools identify finer emotions — frustration, urgency, enthusiasm, anger — and the topics that trigger them.

Frequently asked questions about customer sentiment analysis

What is a customer sentiment analysis tool?

A customer sentiment analysis tool is software that uses natural language processing (NLP) to automatically detect the emotional tone (positive, negative, neutral) and specific emotions in the texts and conversations produced by customers. It lets companies understand how their brand, products and services are perceived at scale — without reading every verbatim by hand.

What is the difference between sentiment analysis and emotion analysis?

Sentiment analysis places a text on a positive/negative/neutral scale. Emotion analysis goes further by identifying precise emotional states — joy, anger, frustration, surprise, disappointment — and their intensity. Advanced tools like custovox.ai combine both: they detect not only whether a customer is unhappy, but why and how strongly.
