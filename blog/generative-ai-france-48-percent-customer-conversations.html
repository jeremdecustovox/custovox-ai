48% of French respondents use generative AI: what it means for your customer conversations

⏱ 6 min read📅 November 12, 2026✍️ custovox.ai

In 2025, 48% of people surveyed in France say they use generative AI, according to ARCEP and CRÉDOC. 42% use it in their private life, 30% for work or studies.

These people are also your customers. They write reviews, open tickets, call your customer service. And they now have a clear idea of what AI can and cannot do.

This article brings together public figures on AI adoption, trust and the AI market in France. Facts are sourced; our interpretations are marked in the “Our reading” boxes.

Fast adoption, and trust that depends on use

Young people drive adoption: 85% of 18-24 year-olds use generative AI, and 73% of 25-39 year-olds, up 35 points from 2023 (ARCEP / CRÉDOC).

Trust follows use. In 2024, nearly 75% of people who have already used AI have high or moderate trust in it. Among those who have never used it, the share drops to 26%.

Concerns remain strong: more than 60% of respondents see AI as a threat to jobs, and 53% see a risk for artistic creation (ARCEP / CRÉDOC 2024).

Our reading

Your customers do not all have the same relationship with AI. Some use it every day and trust it; others are wary. When a company analyzes its customers' messages automatically, that is a case for transparency: say which messages are analyzed, for what purpose, and who reviews the results.

The French language-analysis market: ≈ 6.6x by 2032

According to Statista Market Insights projections, the AI market in France reaches $8.05B in 2025, growing 83.39% over the year. It is expected to add $18.42B by 2032, to ≈ $26.5B.

The most dynamic segment is NLP (automated language analysis), the tools that read and interpret text or speech: $1.77B in 2025, +$9.96B by 2032, to ≈ $11.7B. About 6.6 times more.

Over the same period, machine learning goes from $2.98B to ≈ $7.8B (+$4.78B) and computer vision from $1.45B to ≈ $3.2B (+$1.7B). Across Europe, the AI market is worth $39.77B in 2025 and is expected to grow by $100.06B by 2032.

≈: custovox.ai calculation based on Statista data (2025 value + projected growth to 2032).

What companies ask French AI startups for

According to France Digitale (2024), the first use clients ask French AI startups for is information analysis services. Those clients are mainly large corporations; mid-sized companies come second, with 22%.

The ecosystem is growing: 501 AI startups in 2021, 1,000 expected in 2025, and €1.4B raised in 2025, more than double 2018.

Our reading

Large corporations capture most of the offer. Mid-sized companies also receive reviews, tickets and calls in large numbers, yet remain less served. And information analysis is exactly what AI startups' clients ask for first.

AI to talk to customers, not to listen to them?

In online retail, generative AI is mostly used to produce: 30% of large online retailers use it to create personalized content. The impact of these uses remains hard to measure (Association de l'Économie Numérique, 2024).

Among the general public, the main use is searching for information, cited by nearly half of users. ChatGPT is the most used tool; Mistral AI reaches 6% in January 2025 (CESI / Ipsos 2025).

Our reading

Generating a personalized message is talking to the customer. It does not tell you what they thought of it. The answer already exists: in their reviews, tickets, emails and calls. Measuring the impact of content starts with reading what customers reply.

The risks users identify

Asked about the risks of generative AI, users first mention disinformation. Next come human dependence on these tools and reduced thinking skills, each cited by 44% of respondents (CESI / Ipsos 2025).

Our reading

These concerns also apply to customer-conversation analysis: a summary you cannot check encourages dependence. Automated analysis must stay verifiable.
