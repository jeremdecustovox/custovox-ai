3. Data sent for a case study

To prepare a case study, you send us by email a sample of your customer reviews, tickets or messages. These texts may contain personal data about your customers (names, email addresses, opinions).

  • For this data, you are the data controller and Corpstxt acts as your processor. We process it only on your instructions, for the sole purpose of producing your case study, and never for any other purpose.
  • By sending it, you confirm that you are entitled to share it with us for this purpose. Where possible, please remove or pseudonymize names and contact details first.
  • Only the people at Corpstxt preparing your study have access to it. It is received in our professional mailbox, then analyzed with our own scripts on infrastructure hosted in France; it is never sent to third-party AI providers.
  • It is deleted within 30 days after we deliver your case study (emails and attachments included). You can ask us to delete it earlier at any time.
  • We will inform you without undue delay of any personal data breach affecting it.

If you need a signed data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR), write to privacy@custovox.ai.

4. Cookies and audience measurement

We use no cookies before you choose. When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or refuse audience measurement — refusing is as easy as accepting, and the site works the same either way.

If you accept, we load Google Tag Manager, which runs Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) with Google signals and advertising features turned off. Google Tag Manager itself sets no cookie; Google Analytics sets these cookies:

NameProviderPurposeLifetime
_gaGoogle AnalyticsDistinguishes visitors13 months
_ga_<ID>Google AnalyticsKeeps the session state13 months

Your choice is stored in your browser (local storage, key cvx-consent) for 6 months, after which we ask again. You can change your mind at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page; if you withdraw your consent, the Analytics cookies are deleted.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal and do not load audience measurement.

5. Who receives your data

Your data is accessible only to the Corpstxt team who needs it, and to our service providers acting on our behalf:

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing or for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights.

8. Your rights (European Union and United Kingdom)

Under the GDPR (and UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase your data;
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing done before;
  • (France) give instructions on what happens to your data after your death.

Write to privacy@custovox.ai. We answer within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests) and may ask for proof of identity if there is a reasonable doubt.

You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or with the authority of your country of residence.

10. Marketing emails

We only send news about CustoVox.ai if you ticked the optional box. Every marketing email identifies Corpstxt, includes our postal address and a one-click unsubscribe link, in line with the GDPR, the French Postal and Electronic Communications Code and the US CAN-SPAM Act. Emails about your own request (your case study, our answer to your message) are not marketing emails.

11. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access restricted to the people who need it, and reputable providers. No transmission over the Internet is completely secure; if a breach likely to result in a risk to your rights occurs, we will notify the competent authority and, where required, you.

12. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 (or under 13 in the United States). If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Open-source MCP servers

Our open-source MCP servers published on PyPI (such as custovox-sentiment) run on your own computer. Recent versions send anonymous usage counters (tool used, sentiment label, number of texts, package version, random session ID). The analyzed text is never sent and no personal data is collected. You can turn this off with the environment variable CUSTOVOX_TELEMETRY=0.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services or in the law. The date at the top shows the latest version. If the changes are significant, we will inform you by appropriate means.
